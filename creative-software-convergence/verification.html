What was changed for the portfolio

  • Packaging only: shared appearance script and light/dark/system theme, portfolio navigation bar, footer return link, canonical and social metadata, a strict content-security policy matching the other pages.
  • The report’s nine third-party images were hotlinked to publishers’ servers, which the portfolio’s img-src data: policy would block and which would disclose visitors’ addresses. Recorded: on 2026-10-21 each was fetched from its listed URL (all returned HTTP 200 and valid image data), resized to at most 1,400 px wide and re-encoded as WebP (about 0.7 MB in total), then embedded in the page. Captions, credits and links to the originals are kept. All nine are the publishers’ own documentation, product or tutorial images; no reuse licence was confirmed, and embedding with credit is not a licence. The portfolio owner accepted this approach on 2026-10-21.
  • Report text, survey figures, diagrams and conclusions were not altered, apart from wording that described the images as linked and an edition note recording this change.

Accuracy checks

  • Recorded (2026-10-21, published pages re-read): Sonarworks survey (dated 2026-02-04) — n=1,194, 58 / 38 / 33.9 / 20.9% usage figures, 57.9% and 20.6% sentiment figures all match. Muse Group (dated 2026-04-10) — 1,200 respondents, 78% open, 82% against generation all match.
  • Reported, not re-checked: the March 2026 VJ anecdote (the report states its source URL was not recovered), tool-version captions and the status of each standards body.

Limits

  • Image rights: not established for any of the nine embedded images. Remove an image on request from its rights holder.
  • Browser testing was limited to one Chromium-based browser in this environment.
  • Editorial analysis and the proposed workflow are interpretation, not measured results.
