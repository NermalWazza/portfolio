Privacy

  • This site runs no analytics, cookies, pixels, fingerprinting or scripts for tracking, and it records nothing about people who do not reply.
  • Replying is voluntary. A reply is a public comment shown with your GitHub username and can be seen by anyone. You can edit or delete your own comment on GitHub.
  • Browsing the site and visiting github.com are processed by GitHub under its own privacy statement, which this portfolio does not control. Not replying does not stop GitHub's own platform logging.
  • Please do not post personal or sensitive information in a reply.
