Verdigris and the Golden Path

An original steampunk–cyber–organic character, a rhythm and a Wizard who will not stay still.

Slide 2 / 10

Purpose and direction

Show how a creative character can be designed, animated and sounded entirely offline, and be documented honestly.

  • Original character; no third-party art, fonts or audio.
  • Fiction. Symbols are interpretations, not claims about real events.
  • Prepared as a draft: nothing published without approval.

Slide 4 / 10

The character

Brass cog head, AI-blue lens, copper boiler, green vines.

  • Moods: curious, brewing, alarmed, triumphant — each changes lens colour, gauge, steam, arms and vine growth.
  • Golden path: a slider walks the character along a path that fills with gold.
  • Accessible: every control is a native button or slider with a text status.
Slide 5 / 10

The Wizard

An evolving fictional device in five stages.

Slide 6 / 10

Narrative journey

Smudge → Silhouette → Hat and Flask → Vine Beard → Golden Thread.

  • Each episode pairs a pitfall (unlabelled assumption, trusting the dashboard, speed mistaken for direction) with a brewing incident.
  • The ending is a choice for Verdigris, not an instruction from the Wizard.
Slide 7 / 10

Audiovisual

Synthesised in the browser on request.

  • Web Audio: noise tick, triangle thump, sine blips, golden arpeggio.
  • Tempo 60–140 BPM; lens pulse follows the beat.
  • Never autoplays; stops when the tab is hidden.
  • Limit: audibility was not confirmed by the build agent — please listen.
Slide 8 / 10

Architecture and reusable assets

Everything is one folder of static files.

  • index.html demo · presentation.html deck · verification.html notes · manifest.json/.yaml.
  • Character SVG is reusable via build/build.py (kept with the review package, not published).
  • CSP allows one hashed inline script; no network, no storage.
Slide 9 / 10

Verified vs proposed

What exists today and what does not.

  • Verified: controls, states, offline behaviour, accessibility checks listed in the notes.
  • Proposed: episodic video, recorded music, raster key art, sprite export.
