Verdigris and the Golden Path

A clockwork-organic character who moves between brass machinery, AI-blue signals and green living growth — following one golden path through brewing incidents, pitfalls and a Wizard who keeps changing shape.

What this is. An original fictional character study and interactive demonstration, drawn entirely as inline SVG and animated with CSS and a small script. It works offline, uses no external files and sends nothing anywhere. Symbols such as the golden path are creative interpretations, not claims about real events. Meet Verdigris

Mood

Rhythm

Sound starts only when you press the button. A clock-tick, a steam-boiler thump, AI-blue blips and a golden arpeggio are synthesised in your browser; the lens pulses with the beat.

96 BPM

The Wizard, in five episodes

The Wizard is an evolving fictional storytelling element. Move the slider to reveal the next stage; each episode adds a pitfall or a brewing incident. He is a device for the story, not an authority.

Episode 1 of 5

Brewing lab

Choose ingredients, then brew. Outcomes are fixed, fictional and repeatable.

Visual identity

Steampunk

Brass, copper, clockwork

A cog-shaped head, riveted copper boiler and a pressure gauge that reports mood as steam.

Cyber

AI-blue illumination

One lens eye and a spark on the antenna. Blue means signal; red means a pitfall has been noticed.

Organic

Green growth

Vines extend along the arms as the character brews and retreat when alarmed. Growth is movement.

Golden path

Connection and direction

A creative symbol for joining machine, signal and living form into one chosen direction. Interpretation only.
