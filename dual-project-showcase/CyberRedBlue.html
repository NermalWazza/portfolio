    Harbour Street Bakery under attack

    A fictional small business runs a public web shop and a customer database. Red wants to steal the customer data from APP-DB. Blue must detect, contain and recover — without taking the shop offline. One action each per round, ten rounds.

    Everything here is abstract state in your browser: no scanning, exploits, credentials or network traffic exist. MITRE ATT&CK ids are labels for orientation only; the mechanics are simplified and do not reproduce real attacks.
